Featured Destinations

Uncover the magic of Cambodia through its iconic destinations! Marvel at the ancient wonders of Angkor Wat, feel the pulse of history in Phnom Penh, unwind on the stunning beaches of Koh Rong, and savor the charm of Kampot's lush landscapes. Each destination is a treasure trove of experiences waiting to be explored!

Angkor Wat

Explore the ancient temples and rich history of Angkor Wat, a UNESCO World Heritage site.

Phnom Penh

Experience the vibrant culture and history of Cambodia's capital city, Phnom Penh.

Koh Rong

Relax on the pristine beaches and enjoy the crystal-clear waters of Koh Rong island.

Kampot

Discover the charm of Kampot's lush landscapes and vibrant local culture.

Sihanoukville

Enjoy the beautiful beaches and vibrant nightlife of Sihanoukville.

Battambang

Experience the rich culture and history of Battambang, known for its colonial architecture.

Popular Food

Indulge in the flavors of Cambodia with our selection of popular dishes! From the savory goodness of Amok to the crispy delight of Lok Lak, each dish tells a story of tradition and culture. Don't miss out on the sweet treat of Num Banh Chok and the refreshing taste of Khmer salads!

Amok

A traditional Cambodian dish made with fish, coconut milk, and spices.

Lok Lak

A popular Cambodian stir-fried beef dish served with rice and a tangy sauce.

Num Banh Chok

A traditional Cambodian breakfast dish made with rice noodles and fish gravy.

Kuy Teav

A popular Cambodian noodle soup made with rice noodles and various toppings.

Khmer Salad

A refreshing salad made with fresh vegetables, herbs, and a tangy dressing.

Num Ansom

A traditional Cambodian sticky rice cake filled with banana and mung beans.

Popular Events

Experience the vibrant culture of Cambodia through its popular events! From the colorful celebrations of Khmer New Year to the thrilling Water Festival, each event is a unique opportunity to immerse yourself in the local traditions and festivities. Don't miss out on the excitement of the Angkor Photo Festival and the lively Bon Om Touk!

Bon Om Touk

Cambodian Water Festival, is celebrated in late October or early November

Angkor Photo Festival

A photography festival held annually in Siem Reap, showcasing local and international photographers.

Water Festival

Phnom Penh International Half Marathon

A popular running event held annually in Phnom Penh, attracting participants from around the world.

Khmer New Year

A major celebration in Cambodia, marking the end of the harvest season.

Phnom Penh International Film Festival

A film festival showcasing local and international films, held annually in Phnom Penh.
